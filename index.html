Hampshire Plant Hire — Done Properly

Hampshire Quality Plant Hire is a family-run plant hire business operating across the whole of Hampshire. When you call us, you speak directly with the owner — not a booking team or call centre.

We hire out well-maintained Kubota mini diggers for residential and trade customers who need a machine without the fuss. Whether it's a garden project, drainage run, or groundworks prep, we'll get the right kit to you, when you need it.
